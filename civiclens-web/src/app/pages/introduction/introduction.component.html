Getting Started

1

Create a workspace

Create a workspace to organise your documents and keep everything related to a specific topic or project together.

2

Create a document folder

Create a folder inside your workspace to organize related documents together, making them easier to manage and find later.

3

Upload & embed documents

PlainView processes your documents and creates searchable embeddings so the relevant information can be retrieved when you ask a question.

4

Chat with your documents

Ask questions in plain language and chat with the RAG system to find clear answers grounded in your documents.

Grounded answers

PlainView uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to find relevant information from your documents before generating an answer.
